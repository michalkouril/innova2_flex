LicenseRef-AMD-IP

The bitstream and flash images in images/ were produced by AMD Vivado. Besides the design of the innova2
contributors (Apache-2.0), they contain AMD (Xilinx) IP cores generated by the tool (the DDR4 memory controller (MIG),
XDMA/PCIe, AXI Quad SPI, SmartConnect and clocking). Those cores are provided under the terms of the AMD tool and IP
licences that applied when they were generated (the Xilinx/AMD End User License Agreement shipped with Vivado
2023.2), not under Apache-2.0.

They are redistributed here in Bitstream form only, as section 3(a)(3)C of that EULA permits, and only
for use to program AMD (Xilinx) devices. The generated source/netlist output of the AMD IP is not distributed: the
DDR4 controller ships as its configuration (source/ip/ddr4_flex_ip/ddr4_flex/ddr4_flex.xci), and the build
regenerates it with the user's own AMD Vivado licence.
